## Description

```
In 1900, sponge divers found an ancient shipwreck off the Greek island of Antikythera. Among the statues was a corroded lump of bronze with gears inside: a hand-cranked machine, built more than 2,000 years ago, that calculated the positions of the Sun and Moon, the Moon's phase, and when eclipses were likely. This is how it worked, gear by gear.
```
